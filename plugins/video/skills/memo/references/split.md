# One memo, several shorts

A voice memo is said the way you think: one idea leads to the next, you come back to the first, you
add something "oh, and I forgot". Inside a long one there are often **several videos**. One short
per idea multiplies what you can post, and nobody watches a long short (Ihsane, 2026-10-07: "il y a
peut-être plusieurs vidéos que je peux faire, short de 1 minute, 2 minutes, 3 minutes, au lieu d'en
faire une seule très grande").

## What makes a short

A short is **one point a viewer can repeat in one sentence** after watching (its `core`), with
what the memo says to back it: an example, a reason, a story, a how-to. Read the whole memo first,
then list the points; each becomes a short when:

- it stands alone: a viewer who sees only this one understands it, without the others;
- the memo says enough about it for 30 s at least (not one passing sentence);
- it is the user's own: their experience, their opinion, their method. The substance is theirs.

A memo of 1 to 3 minutes is usually **one** short. A memo of 10 minutes may hold 4 to 8. Never cut
a point into two shorts to make more; never merge two points into one short to make it longer.

## The whole and the parts

When the memo walks through a process or a list (steps, tips, a system), it gives two kinds of
shorts, and both are worth keeping:

- **the whole**: the overview, every step in one line, for the viewer who wants the map;
- **the parts**: one short per step that the memo develops enough, each with its own hook.

The whole and each part are different videos: they share words, not their point.

## The pieces that move

- **An afterthought** ("ah, j'ai oublié…", "il faudra modifier ça au montage") belongs to the point
  it completes: put its range in that short's `from`, not in a short of its own.
- **The opening and the closing** of a memo (why I record this, "tell me what you think", the call to
  action) are not shorts. The lead magnet goes at the end of every version anyway (versions.md).
- **Asides to the editor** ("cut this", "say it better in the edit") are instructions, not content:
  follow them, never put them in a script.
- A **tangent** that is a full idea of its own (said with conviction, with a reason) is a short; a
  tangent said in passing is not.

## The order

Number the shorts in the order of the memo (`s1`, `s2`…): the interface shows them in that order.
Name the one you would shoot first in `first` (its id), and say why in the memo's `summary`, in one
sentence: the Studio offers it as the next thing to do.

## Lengths

From the profile (`lengths`): 30 s to 3 min, most between 40 and 90 s. A point that needs more
than 3 minutes is two points. A version is measured at the user's pace by `memo.mjs check`: write,
check, cut or add, check again.
