# The scrapbook language, measured

Two edits live here:

1. **The reference**: the edit the language was taken from: a 72.7 s vertical list video
   (« 7 Claude skills that turn your Claude into a creative studio, comment "skills" for the full
   install »), edited by the user for a client, 720×1280 at 30 fps, −14.5 LUFS. The video itself is
   the client's and is not kept here: what follows is its anatomy, measured frame by frame (30 fps
   sheets, face tracking) and on its spectrum (tones tracked partial by partial, levels against the
   voice), on 2026-10-04.
2. **The development cut** (`plan.mjs`, `edl.mjs`, `project.json` in this folder): the skill
   rebuilding that language on another take (50.4 s of a sales rep, Pipecorn, Station F), used to
   tune every figure, and **VALIDATED by the user on 2026-10-05** (v3.5, « ce style de montage est
   validé »): the look the skill reproduces (`workspace/videos/scrapbook-dev-sales.mp4`).
   `__ENGINE__` / `__SKILL__` stand for the two scripts folders.

## The development cut

`plan.mjs` here is the skill's edit of a 50.4 s take (a sales rep, Pipecorn, Station F): 3.27 s of
pauses cut (16 stretches kept, 15 jump cuts), 47.1 s out. `__ENGINE__` / `__SKILL__` stand for the
two scripts folders.

**v1 (2026-10-04) was refused in review**: flat paper labels, a synthesised sound kit, memes placed
off the face. **v2 (2026-10-05)** re-measured the paper scrap by scrap (breathing), put the memes at
the face cut in and out, real paper sounds and the house pack; on its boards the user picked the
unfold and « Paper slide » at 19 LU under the voice. **v2 was refused too** (SKILL.md § The reviews:
the fonts, the colours, not dynamic, no shadows, the GIFs placed at random). **v3** brought Archivo,

What v3.2 taught:
- **The paper is punctuation, not a font.** The reference put paper on its titles and on a few
  words; v2 to v3.1 put it on every keyword, under captions that already said them.
- **Test the important word's colour on the take.** Coral (the reference's) and orange vanished in
  the speaker's hair and face; yellow read everywhere.
- **A row field renamed is a silent bug.** The marker's strike read `row.asc`, a field the
  reference's renderLabel no longer kept: NaN, nothing drawn, and the render still conformed
  (verify checks sound and picture, not a missing stroke). A test now draws the strike.

What v3.1 taught:
- **A measured paper is not a style choice to improve on.** v3 « improved » the reference's scraps
  (torn pieces, fibre, crease, tape, light and shade across them): the user wanted the reference's
  own scraps back, and no gradient on any paper. Motion, face and shadows can move away from a
  reference; its paper could not.
- **The bed is measured as a median, the sounds as peaks.** `measure.mjs` reads the bed's momentary
  median on the bus (15 LU under the voice), a sound's loudest momentary (19-23 LU under).

What v3 taught (each is now in the scripts):
- **Motion blur must average whole instants.** Six samples of a figure laid with « lighter » add up
  right only if each sample is ONE drawImage: a shadow and a sprite in the same sample add their
  alphas. The soft shadow is painted once, under, and only the sprite is sampled (`drawMoving`).
- **A slap is relative to the size.** A 1.55 × bounce is right for a sticker; on a strip 0.75 W
  wide it covered the frame: `amp` (0.32 for the hook's promise).
- **Red on hair disappears.** An arrow drawn over the head read as a squiggle: a white halo under a
  marker drawn on the picture (not on paper).
- **A white paper on a white wall needs its shadow**, not a darker paper: the soft shadow (36 %, 0.019 W)
  is what the second review asked for.

What v2 taught:
- **The engine's layers were judder.** The overlay layers were FFV1 in Matroska, which keeps time
  in whole milliseconds: a frame at 2/30 s was stored at 0.067 s, after the picture's 0.0667, and
  the overlay showed the previous layer frame on every third frame and never the one drawn (11
  wrong frames out of 31 on a synthetic test). An entrance in 4 frames held its first frame twice.
  `build.sh` writes them in NUT now (exact 1/30 time base): 0 wrong frames.
- **Measure a sound on the effects bus, not between two masters.** Subtracting two finished mixes
  left the voice 21-24 LU under the programme (the dynamic loudnorm does not give two mixes the same
  curve): every click vanished in it. `measure.mjs` subtracts on the bus, before the loudness pass.
- **The house levels were measured, not copied.** The README of the pack says « click 12-16 LU »
  as heard; measured this way on the style-01 cut of the same take, the validated click is 25.3 LU
  under the voice. The figures in `book.mjs SOUNDS` are those measurements.
- **A cut copy has its own loudness.** The pencil cut to half a second of typing plays 5 dB under
  the whole file: each cut copy is measured (`<name>-<ms>.json`).
- **A meme centred on the frame is right only when the speaker is.** The reference's face sat at
  0.46-0.50 and its memes at 0.50; this speaker sits at 0.32-0.58: `F.meme()` follows the face
  past 0.06 off the centre line.

From v1, still true:
- **Cut in whole frames, into a file** (`tighten.py` → `source/tight.mov`): cutting on the fly put
  the lips 2 frames behind the voice, and a project with cuts cannot be rendered by slices.
- **Whisper is early in a silence**: an onset in room tone moves to where the speech starts.
- **A figure handed no frame draws in the project's** (FRAME), never 1080×1920 on a 720×1280 take.

## The reviews, in the user's words

The six reviews that settled the look (SKILL.md § The user's decisions keeps the rules they left).

**v1 (2026-10-04), refused** (« très mauvaise qualité »): « there is no paper effect on the
elements; the paper unfolds a little, frame by frame there is a little animation », « a little
paper sound when it arrives, coordinated, very pro », « all the sound effects are bad: take what the
other styles have, then the paper », « even where the GIFs are placed is not good ». → v2: the
paper measured scrap by scrap and breathing, real paper recordings and the house pack, memes centred.
On the boards with v2, the user picked how a paper arrives (**B: it unfolds** in 4 frames) and the
paper sound (**3, « Paper slide »**, at 19 LU under the voice). Both are still the rule (the unfold
lives inside `toss`).

**v2 (2026-10-05), refused** (« absolument pas qualitatif »): « the fonts are not right, the colours
are not right, it is not dynamic »; « the animated labels are not beautiful at all: no shadow behind
them, so the contrast does not work » (white paper on a white wall); « take inspiration from an
edit made in After Effects, a pro tool: little shadows behind the texts, behind the elements », not
copying the fourth style; « a new SFX pack to complete the little sounds » (Motionix); « the GIFs
are placed far too randomly, they have no nice entrance »; « take more liberty with the reference
to make it exceptional — but keep the paper effect; you can improve it ». → v3: one face

**v3 (2026-10-05), half kept** (« c'est déjà mieux »): « j'aime beaucoup la motion, les
